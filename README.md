# The Legend of Mir 1 — Carbon Source

**An open-source remake of ActozSoft's original 1997 MMORPG, The Legend of Mir.**

Carbon is a community-driven project dedicated to preserving, restoring, and developing the original Mir 1 experience. By making the source code, client downloads, server database, documentation, and development resources accessible, the project gives the community an opportunity to explore the foundations of one of Korea's early MMORPGs.

## About the Project

Originally launched in 1997 by ActozSoft, **The Legend of Mir 1** was an early Korean MMORPG that helped establish the foundations of a franchise that would continue through Mir 2, Mir 3, and subsequent generations.

Set in a persistent 2D fantasy world inspired by Eastern mythology, Mir 1 brought together exploration, monster hunting, character progression, equipment, trading, player-versus-player combat, and the social dynamics of a shared online world.

Its mechanics were shaped by the technical limitations and design ambitions of its era. Despite its relative obscurity outside its original markets, Mir 1 remains an important part of the history of the Legend of Mir series.

### Preserving Mir's Origins

Carbon aims to keep Mir 1 accessible to the community by providing a foundation for running, studying, and developing the game.

The project brings together source code, server resources, client downloads, database resources, and community-maintained documentation to make getting started easier and encourage further development.

This is more than an opportunity to revisit an old MMORPG. It is a chance to preserve a piece of gaming history, understand how the original game worked, and build upon the work of the developers and community members who came before us.
